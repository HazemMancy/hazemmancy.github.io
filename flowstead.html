Flowstead

An open-source process simulator for Android and the web, built on the DWSIM thermodynamic engine: steady-state and dynamic flowsheets, relief and line sizing, and engineering deliverables. Install it on a phone, or open it in any browser with nothing to install.

WHY I BUILT IT

A real simulation engine, in your pocket

Much of process engineering is quick checking: a flash, a line, a relief case. Flowstead lets you build, solve and check a flowsheet on a phone, or in a browser on an iPhone, iPad or computer, with every number coming from the open-source DWSIM engine rather than shortcut correlations. The Android app runs offline and the web version runs on your own device with no server, and it is open source, so anyone can read how a result was produced.

My role: creator and technical owner. I defined the product, set the engineering rules it follows, verified its results against HYSYS and released it.

FEATURES

What it does

Simulation

  • Steady-state and dynamic flowsheets
  • Distillation columns and absorbers
  • Reactors
  • Pipelines (Beggs & Brill)
  • Pump and compressor performance curves
  • Phase envelopes with hydrate curves and water dew lines

Safety & Relief

  • PSV sizing (API 520 / 526)
  • Relief scenarios and depressuring (API 521: fire, blocked outlet)
  • PSV inlet-loss and back-pressure checks

Sizing

  • Separator and line sizing (API 14E)
  • Control valve sizing

Outputs

  • Equipment data sheets
  • Heat and material balances
  • PFDs
  • Export to PDF and Excel

VERIFICATION

How results are checked

Results are checked in automated tests against HYSYS cases, so a change that moves a number away from the reference fails the build before it can be released. For example, the fire-case PSV for a wet air receiver reproduces the orifice that HYSYS selects for the same case (1D2).
